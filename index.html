Questions

Before you call.

How much does tree work cost?

It depends on tree size, species, access, and whether you need removal, pruning, grinding, or all three. That’s why quotes are free — send photos or a description and we’ll price it straight.

Do you remove trees or only grind stumps?

Both. We’re a full-service tree company — removal, pruning, storm cleanup, and stump grinding. Tell us what you need and we’ll scope the whole job.

How far down do you grind stumps?

Typically below grade so you can reseed, sod, or landscape over the spot. If you have a specific depth in mind, put it in the form.

Do you haul chips and brush away?

Grinding and chipping leave material on site. Many customers keep chips as mulch. If you want brush or chips hauled or spread, say so in the quote request.

Do you handle storm damage?

Yes. Fallen limbs, blocked drives, and wind-damaged trees are part of what we do. Put “storm” or “emergency” in the form so we can prioritize.

Will the equipment wreck my lawn?

There will be a work area — that’s the job. Our commercial equipment is built to get into yards and get the work done, not tear up the whole property. Mention fences, slopes, or tight gates so we can plan access.

Do I need to be home?

Not always, as long as the trees or stumps are accessible and you leave clear instructions. Put access notes in the form (gate codes, dogs, where to park).

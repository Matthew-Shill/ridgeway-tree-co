Questions

Before you call.

How much does stump grinding cost?

It depends on stump size, species, count, and how hard the machine is to get in. That’s why quotes are free — send photos or a description and Dewey will price it straight.

How far down do you grind?

Typically below grade so you can reseed, sod, or landscape over the spot. If you have a specific depth in mind, put it in the form.

Do you haul the chips away?

Grinding turns the stump into chips on site. Many customers keep them as mulch. If you want them hauled or spread, say so in the quote request.

Will the machine wreck my lawn?

There will be a work area — that’s the job. The newer commercial machine is built to get into yards and grind the stump, not tear up the whole property. Mention fences, slopes, or tight gates so Dewey can plan access.

Do I need to be home?

Not always, as long as the stumps are accessible and you leave clear instructions. Put access notes in the form (gate codes, dogs, where to park).
